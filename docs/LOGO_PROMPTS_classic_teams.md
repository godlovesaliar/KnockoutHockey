# Logo prompts — classic teams (2026-11-24)

The Boston Beacon and the Narragansett Anchor are back as the two classic teams, in
leather helmets and gauntlets, so their crests should look like they came off a wool
sweater from the 1920s to 1940s, not like the rest of the league's modern logos.

Each block below is a whole prompt: paste the **vintage style** block first, then one
team block. The team blocks use the same fields as the earlier rounds. Colors are the
teams' uniform colors as the game has them (`scripts/core/teams.gd`).

Picked and cut in on 2026-11-24: the round patch for the Beacon and the diamond patch
for the Anchor (`assets/images/logo_beacon.jfif`, `assets/images/logo_anchor.jfif`, the
last two lines of `SHEETS` in `tools/build_logos.gd`). The two letter logos were not
used.

---

## Vintage style (paste before either team)

```
Design a single sports team logo in the style of a vintage ice hockey sweater crest
from the 1920s to 1940s: the kind cut from felt or stitched in chenille and sewn onto
a heavy wool sweater.

Style rules:
- Flat, solid shapes only. No gradients, no shading, no highlights, no shine, no 3D,
  no drop shadows, no texture, no distressing or fake wear.
- Simple, chunky, slightly hand-cut forms, as if each color were its own piece of
  felt. Few details; every shape big enough to cut out with scissors.
- Old-fashioned and dignified, not aggressive. No modern sports-logo speed lines,
  swooshes, bevels, sharp angular mascots or motion effects.
- Only the three colors listed. Cream takes the place of white inside the logo.
- One uniform black outline around the whole logo and between its color areas, the
  same thickness everywhere.
- No hockey sticks, no pucks, no waves, no swirls, no shields, no banners, no stars.
- No words, no team name, no city name, no numbers, no year. A single letter only
  if the team block asks for one.
- One logo, centered, on a plain pure white background, with clear empty space all
  around it. Nothing else in the image.
```

## Boston Beacon

```
Team: Boston Beacon
Colors: Harbor Navy #0F1A3D, Lamp Gold #E0B347, Cream #F2E6C7. No other colors.
Logo type: crest logo (a round felt patch)
Subject: A round patch. A thick lamp gold ring forms the border, with a solid harbor
navy disc inside it. Standing in the middle of the disc, a simple cream lighthouse
seen straight on: a plain tapering tower with two navy bands across it, a small
lantern room on top with a little pointed gold cap. From the lantern, two broad lamp
gold beams fan out, one to the left and one to the right, each a plain flat wedge that
widens until it meets the gold ring. The tower stands on a low cream mound of rock at
the bottom of the disc. Perfectly symmetrical. No sea, no clouds, no stars.
Expression: none (no face).
```

Alternate, if the round patch comes out too busy:

```
Team: Boston Beacon
Colors: Harbor Navy #0F1A3D, Lamp Gold #E0B347, Cream #F2E6C7. No other colors.
Logo type: letter logo
Subject: A large, heavy, old-fashioned block capital B with square slab serifs, in
lamp gold, as if cut from a single piece of felt. Standing in front of the upright
stroke of the B and a little taller than the letter, a simple cream lighthouse seen
straight on: a plain tapering tower with two navy bands and a small navy lantern room
on top. No beams of light, no sea.
Expression: none (no face).
```

Colors: Boxcar Red #8C2B21, Cream #F0DEB3, Black #141212. No other colors.

wheels under it, sitting on a single straight cream rail that runs the width of the
field. No locomotive, no other cars, no smoke, no lettering on the car.
