## Team logos

The team logos (`assets/images/teamlogos.jfif`, `logos2.webp` through `logos6.jfif`) and the roster sheet (`data/knockout_hockey_rosters.csv`) were supplied by the project owner; `tools/build_logos.gd` cuts them into `assets/characters/logo_<team>.png`.

## Sound

The recordings in `assets/sound/` (shots, hits, hockey stops, skating, goal horn, crowd, cheers, boos, chants, organ) and the announcer lines in `assets/sound/announcer/` are cut into the clips in `assets/sound/cut/` by `tools/cut_sounds.py`. All of them were generated with ElevenLabs (sound effects).

## Characters and animations

Y Bot and all 51 animations in `assets/animations/` are from [Mixamo](https://www.mixamo.com) (Adobe). They're free to use in personal and commercial projects; no attribution is required. They're credited here anyway.
